## Signal Summary

Knowledge distillation techniques enable compressing large AI models into smaller, faster variants while preserving accuracy. The article highlights the pain points of deploying large models in production: high latency, memory cost, and infrastructure requirements.

## Key Bullets from Source

- Large deep learning models are slow and require high memory/compute
- Knowledge distillation transfers learned behavior from a "teacher" model to a smaller "student" model

- Applicable to classification, NLP, and image processing tasks
